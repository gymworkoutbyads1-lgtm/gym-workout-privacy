About this policy

This policy explains how Gym and Workout by ads (also displayed as Gym & Workout by ADS or E.V.O.R.A.) handles information in the current Android app.

For privacy questions or support, contact the app developer at gym.workout.by.ads.1@gmail.com.

1. Information you enter

The app uses profile details, goals, training preferences, workouts, sets, reps, weights, meal and water logs, weigh-ins, measurements and selected photos to provide its fitness features. Optional details can include date of birth, gender and height.

Fitness records and photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a fitness cloud service. Signing in does not sync or back up these records.

2. Accounts and sign-in

If you create or sign into an account, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email, display name, account identifier and sign-in credentials. Google sign-in uses the account details you authorize. Authentication services also process technical information, such as IP addresses and user-agent information, to operate and protect sign-in.

The app does not save your password in its fitness records. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Barcode scanning and nutrition lookup

Google Code Scanner processes camera images on your device and returns a barcode. Google states that it does not store scanner images or results. The scanner SDK collects technical diagnostics and usage data, including device and app information, identifiers, performance and error events. Auto-zoom generates session and zoom diagnostics.

For nutrition lookup, the app sends the product barcode to Open Food Facts over HTTPS. That service receives the request, your IP address and an app identifier. The request does not include your account email, fitness history or meal photos. You can enter food details manually instead.

See Google Code Scanner, scanner data disclosures and Open Food Facts privacy information.

4. Photos and permissions

The Android file picker gives the app access to photos or backup files you select. Profile and progress photos are copied into private app storage. The app does not request contacts, microphone, location or entire-photo-library access. Google Play services handles the barcode camera interface.

Notification and alarm permissions support reminders and timers. Vibration provides enabled feedback. Reminders may be rescheduled after a restart or time change. You can change notification permissions in Android settings and reminder preferences in Profile.

5. Storage, security and backups

The app uses Android private storage and HTTPS for its configured online requests. Protect your phone with a screen lock. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Automatic app backup is disabled. Manual ZIP exports include fitness records, settings and selected photos, up to 1 GB. These exports are not password-protected or encrypted by the app. Anyone with the ZIP may read it. Store it securely and share it only with people or storage providers you trust. Restore replaces the current records for the active profile.

6. Retention and in-app deletion

Local records remain until you delete them using available entry controls, replace them by restoring a backup, clear app storage or uninstall. Signing out does not erase records. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete your online sign-in account.

To delete a signed-in account, open Profile → Account & sign-in → Delete account and verify your identity. This removes the Firebase account and its associated records on that phone. Separate guest records, other local accounts, photos still used by those records, exported files and copies on other devices are not erased by this action. Remove those copies separately.

A local deleted-account identifier is retained to prevent old records from being restored automatically; clearing app storage removes it. Firebase documents that logged IP addresses are retained for a few weeks and other authentication information is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days after account deletion is initiated.

7. Your choices

You can edit your profile and goals, manage reminder permissions, delete supported log entries, export records or delete your account from Profile. Hide personal data masks supported displays; it does not delete or encrypt records.

To remove all local profiles and guest records, use Android's Clear storage option for this app. Export anything you want to keep first. External backups must be deleted at their saved location.

8. Ads, subscriptions and service providers

The current version does not show ads or accept subscription payments. Pro and Ad-Free prices are previews only. No advertising SDK or separate app analytics service is configured. Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication and the scanner still process the service data described above.

Providers may process information outside your country under their privacy policies. This policy will be updated before introducing ads, billing or cloud fitness backup.

9. This website

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information when you visit, including IP addresses for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. Email links open your email application; no message is sent until you send it.

10. Fitness guidance

The app supports fitness and nutrition tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Workout suggestions and nutrition values are not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice or before changes that may affect a health condition.

11. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised date. For questions about the app or this policy, email gym.workout.by.ads.1@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
