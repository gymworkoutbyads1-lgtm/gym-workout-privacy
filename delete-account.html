Send a deletion request

Email gym.workout.by.ads.1@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request — Gym and Workout by ads.

Send from the email used to sign in to the app when possible. Include that account email if it differs. Do not send your password, verification codes, health records or identity documents.

Email deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address into your preferred email service. Opening the email draft does not send your request.

What happens next

  1. The developer reviews the request and may reply to verify that you own the account.
  2. After verification, the developer removes the Firebase sign-in account and associated remotely held account data.
  3. You receive confirmation when the request is completed, or an explanation if more information is needed.

Requests are handled manually, not instantly by this website. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion. If you cannot access your account email, contact the same support address for help verifying ownership.

What is deleted and what may remain

Account deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account, including its email, display name and sign-in identity. The app does not upload workouts, nutrition logs, measurements or progress photos to a fitness cloud database.

Fitness records stored on your devices cannot be remotely erased through an email request. To remove all local records, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Delete exported ZIP backups and copies saved with other providers separately. Separate guest records or other users' records are not part of the requested account deletion.

Firebase states that logged IP addresses are retained for a few weeks, and other authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated. A deleted-account marker created by in-app deletion may remain locally until app storage is cleared.

Request emails may remain in the developer's support mailbox while the request is handled and to document its completion; they are separate from your fitness records. You can ask for deletion of the related support correspondence as part of your request.

You can also delete inside the app

Open Profile → Account & sign-in → Delete account and complete identity verification. This removes the sign-in account and its associated local records on that phone. Photos shared with other local profiles, guest records, backups and data on other devices require separate removal.

Deletion is permanent. Export anything you want to keep before using the in-app deletion option. Signing out alone does not delete your account.
